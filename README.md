## 🌟 Featured Project: Space Antigravity Adventure (Kids 4–6)

<div align="center">
  <h3>🚀 Space Antigravity Adventure (Ages 4–6)</h3>
  <p><b>An active zero-gravity shape matching & sorting mission designed for intentional motor skills and early math!</b></p>
  
  <a href="https://game-kids-4-6.onrender.com/">
    <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/▶_LAUNCH_LIVE_GAME-https%3A%2F%2Fgame--kids--4--6.onrender.com-blue?style=for-the-badge&logo=google-chrome&logoColor=white" alt="Play Game" height="40" />
  </a>
</div>

<br/>

### ✨ Highlights:
- 🌌 **Zero-Gravity Sorting**: Shapes float gently upward in low gravity and are dragged into matching holographic collection pods.
- 🎯 **Tactile Motor Skills**: Oversized hitboxes with magnetic tractor beam rings designed specifically for developing coordination.
- 🎆 **Rewarding Feedback**: Stardust confetti bursts, floating `+1 ⭐`, and cheerful synthesized chime chords on every match.
- 🛡️ **Zero Frustration**: Gentle rubbery "boing" wobble returns shapes safely to the play area on mismatch.
- 🚀 **Rocket Blastoff Victory**: Filling the 5-star progress meter launches rockets into space with celebration fanfare and automatic round reset.
- 🎶 **Native Web Audio Synthesizer**: 100% self-contained procedural sound effects with zero external audio dependencies.
- ⚡ **Powered by Go**: Ultra-fast, lightweight Go standard library backend running smoothly on Render.
